# Responsible Use Policy

RakamLah can automate browser actions, synthesize speech, and produce convincing media. Use it transparently and with
respect for the people represented by a recording.

## Voice and identity

- Obtain informed permission before adding a person's voice as a synthesis reference.
- Do not use a real or synthetic voice to deceive, defraud, harass, or falsely attribute statements to someone.
- Disclose synthetic narration when an audience could reasonably mistake it for a real speaker.
- Do not bypass platform rules, privacy rights, publicity rights, or applicable law.

TolongLabs has authorized the bundled samples for the uses described in [the media terms](media/LICENSE.md). That
authorization does not extend to samples users add themselves.

## Browser recordings

- Use dedicated test accounts and synthetic data whenever possible.
- Mask credentials, private messages, personal data, tokens, and other secrets before capture.
- Do not automate access you are not authorized to perform.
- Review the final artifact before publication; automation does not transfer editorial responsibility.

Reports of harmful or deceptive use may be raised through the channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md), as appropriate.
